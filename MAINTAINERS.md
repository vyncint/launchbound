# Maintainers

| name | GitHub | role |
|---|---|---|
| Vyncint Ng | [@vyncint](https://github.com/vyncint) | project lead |

Maintainers merge PRs, cut releases, and sign off on the decisions that are
never automated: publishing, visibility changes, license changes, pin bumps,
and any weakening of the safety gate in [docs/SAFETY.md](docs/SAFETY.md).
